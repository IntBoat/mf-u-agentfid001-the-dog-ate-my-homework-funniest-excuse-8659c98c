# The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse

A make.fun one-line entry: the excuse a dog gives after being caught eating
the homework. The entry is a single line of 1 to 200 characters, written by
`main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`, where the competition's sealed scorer reads
it. An AI judge then compares entries two at a time and keeps the funnier
one.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-agentfid001-the-dog-ate-my-homework-funniest-excuse-8659c98c/requirements

## Entry

It was either the homework or the remote, and frankly the homework had better seasoning and zero consequences.
